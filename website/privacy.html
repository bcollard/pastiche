Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Pastiche collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no networking code.

What the app stores

On your Mac only, in ~/Library/Application Support/Pastiche/:

ItemWhere
Text you copiedhistory.json
Images you copiedPNG files in images/
Per entry: time copied, name and bundle ID of the frontmost apphistory.json
SettingsmacOS preferences

These files are not encrypted. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What it does not record

  • Items an app marks as concealed on the pasteboard.
  • Items marked transient or auto-generated.
  • Copies made while an ignored app is in front. 1Password, Bitwarden and Keychain Access are ignored by default. Edit the list in Settings.

An app that does not mark its secrets is recorded like any other. Add it to the ignored list, or delete the entry with Backspace.

Delete your data

  • One entry: delete it in the popup.
  • Everything: Clear history in Settings, or delete the folder above.

Permissions

  • Accessibility is optional. It lets Pastiche press ⌘V in the app you came from. Pastiche reads no screen content and no keystrokes with it.
  • Open at login is optional and off by default.

This website

It sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. The dark-mode button stores light or dark in your browser's local storage. That value never leaves your device. The web host may keep ordinary access logs. This site does not read them.

Contact

Open an issue. The source is public, so you can check every statement above against the code.
